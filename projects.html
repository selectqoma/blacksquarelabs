AI integration

Connect your tools, data and workflows around an agentic core. From the first use case to deployment and handover, get the engineering to make AI part of how your business works.

Built around how you work

Coordinate work across systems

Build an orchestration layer that brings together context, agents and existing workflows. Define which actions it can take, when work needs approval and how it handles exceptions.

Ask in everyday language

Give your team a conversational way to find information and initiate work. A request can gather account context, prepare a proposal or start an onboarding workflow, with results returned in one place.

Connect the tools you use

Bring CRM, email, documents and internal systems into the workflow. Use available connectors, APIs and Model Context Protocol (MCP) where supported, with custom integrations for your business.

From workflow
to working system

Start with a business process and the tools it touches. Agree the scope, required access and success criteria before building. If you already have code, a Technical Review can help identify what to reuse.
