AI built around your business

Blacksquare Labs is an independent AI consulting and engineering practice based in Brussels. We connect business tools, data and workflows so AI can become part of everyday work.

From business process
to working software

The starting point is how your team works: what it needs to know, which tools it uses and where work gets held up.

We build an agentic core around those workflows, with natural language interaction and connections to the systems you already rely on. That means mapping the process, integrating the tools, building the software and getting it into daily use.

You work directly with an engineer on both the code and the technical decisions. Bring Blacksquare Labs in for a defined integration project or as an ongoing partner to guide AI adoption and improve the system as your needs change.

The code stays in your repositories. Your team receives the documentation and practical handover needed to operate and maintain the integrations.
