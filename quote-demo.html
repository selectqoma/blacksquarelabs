What does a supplier need to prepare a quote?

The product, quantity, intended use and delivery region. This form shows those details coming together in a sample request.

Choose a product and fill in the details below. You can then create and download a sample request. Nothing is sent to a supplier, and no order is placed.

This is a form example, not a live AI conversation. It illustrates a bounded workflow: validated input, a visible review step and explicit human approval.
