Preparing a weekly
client update

In this example, AI gathers CRM records, documents and emails into a draft update. It includes source links and flags missing information. Your team reviews it before sending.

Workflow steps +

  1. Gather context

    Find this week’s client activity in approved CRM records, documents and emails.

  2. Plan the update

    Identify changes, open questions and next steps for each client.

  3. Check the sources

    Draft the brief with source links and flag gaps or conflicting information.

  4. Review and send

    Let your team check the draft and approve any updates before they are sent.

Frequently asked questions

Which tools can you connect?+

CRM, email, documents, support platforms and internal software. We check the available APIs and access permissions before agreeing the scope.

Do we need to replace our existing software?+

Usually not. We build integrations around your existing systems and agree any required changes before development.

What does a project include?+

Workflow design, integrations, development, testing, deployment and handover. Maintenance can be arranged separately.

Who controls what the AI can do?+

Your team defines the permissions. The application enforces access rules and records actions. Sending emails or changing records can require human approval.

Where do our code and data stay?+

We can work in your repositories and infrastructure. Before development, we agree where data is stored and processed, which model providers are used and who has access.

Who will we work with?+

You work directly with an independent engineer at Blacksquare Labs, from scoping to deployment.
