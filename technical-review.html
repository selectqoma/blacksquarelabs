Technical review

Understand how your existing AI system works across code, data and connected tools. Get an independent review of its architecture, permissions and tests, with clear priorities for what to change.

Code, connections
and behavior

Bring your application or prototype and show the workflows it supports. The review traces requests through the code, model calls and integrations, checking how the system handles context, permissions, failures and handoffs to people.

The review does not include implementation. Your team can use the findings directly, or request a separate AI integration project.

Deliverables

  • Written findingsFindings from the code review and test results, with examples of the problems found.
  • Issues in priority orderWhich problems to fix first and why.
  • A test planTest cases, expected results and checks to run after changes. Implementing the tests is separate work.
  • Recommended fixesWhat to keep, refactor or replace, with dependencies and effort estimates.
  • A review meetingDiscuss the findings and answer questions with your technical team.

What the review requires

Before starting

An existing codebase, a demonstration of the system and a conversation about your workflows and plans. AI-generated code is welcome. You do not need an engineering team, and the review can take place within your environment.
